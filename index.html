What we do

Most research stalls in the same two places: the design was never powered for the question, or the data arrived and nobody had time to make it a result. We take projects at either point and stay through publication.

Study design and power

Sample-size justification, power and sensitivity analysis, and the statistical sections your IRB protocol and grant application need, written to survive review.

Analysis and reporting

From analysis plan to final results. Rigorous methods, publication-ready tables and figures, and findings reported to journal standards in language your team can defend to reviewers and leadership.

Manuscripts and grants

Methods and results sections drafted for submission, statistical responses to peer review, and a named statistician for your grant and program budgets so funded work keeps moving.

Predictive models and program analytics

Risk and outcome models built to current reporting standards, validated honestly, and delivered with the documentation and workflow fit that let a program actually use them.

Who we serve

Institutional investigators, research directors, and clinical research programs, from a first nurse-led study to multi-site work. Our experience spans orthopedics, nursing, emergency medical services, pharmacy, pediatrics, and diagnostic medicine.

Research programs work with us on a standing basis, so a completed study is not a one-time event. Individual investigators work with us on a single project at a time. Both begin with the same conversation.
